The story

The Path

The wreck

A storm out of a clear sky broke your ship, and the sea left you on a small island off a coast no chart shows. You have nothing, and you remember less: not your tools, not your craft, not even how to light a fire. On the beach an old man keeps one burning. He calls himself the Wayfinder, and he has been waiting for castaways like you.

The island

A fishing village lives round the bay. Old Tom fishes off the jetty, Corwin buys your catch, Halvard the boatwright trades in timber and flint, and Kestrel hands out your first hunting task. Knap flint, carve your first tools, earn your first coins, and gather the pages of the captain's log the tide brings in. Tobin the deckhand made it too.

The mainland

When you're ready, the ferry jetty takes you across the water to Landfall, a town of castaways who stayed: Brannoc at the forge, Wren with food and rope, Aldous in his tower, and Vesk paying for monsters.

The seals

This world once knew everything: iron and gold, the Nether's fire, the void past the End. It built faster than the land could bear, and broke. The few who were left sealed every age away, to be learned again by someone who earns it. That road is the Path: forge each seal's key, turn it in the Sealgate, bring down what comes out, and the next act opens. With every seal, a little of who you were comes back.

Tobin saw them from the crow's nest, before the mast went: roofs, north-east of the fires, past the dunes. A town. On no chart I own. Captain's Log, part 3 of 3

    The Seal of the Eyes wants more than a key: the End portal takes twelve eyes, and none of them are sold. They're hidden in ghost ships, ice mazes and cursed pyramids, and carried by bosses like the Warden, the Leviathan and the Wither.

    Common to Mythic

    Gear rarity

    Every piece of gear rolls a rarity. Rarer gear is stronger, lasts longer and carries bonuses.

    Iron Chestplate ★ Epic Hardened When on Body: +8.1 Armor +1 Armor Toughness Requires Combat 16 Durability: 336 / 336 Forged by AK

    An Iron Chestplate at each rarity. Pick a tier to see it.

    Where it comes from

    • Crafting: your skill caps it. Rare from level 10, Epic from 25, Legendary from 45.
    • Chests: rarer finds in dangerous places like strongholds, bastions and ancient cities.
    • Bosses: always Epic or better. The main road to Mythic.
    • Fortune's Dice: a lucky roll can lift what you hold a tier, or make it Mythic.

    Reforging

    Bring gear to Brannoc, or to a Reforging Anvil, and roll its rarity again for coins. It never comes out worse. Smithing tips the odds, and Mythic is a one-in-a-thousand strike. Mythic weapons heal you for a tenth of the damage they deal.

    Coin and chance

    Coins, Bounties & Dice

    Earn coins, take on bounties and hunts, and test your luck.

    Coins

    • Sell logs, fibre, rocks and produce to Wren, flint and coal to Brannoc.
    • Spend them in the town's shops, on reforging and in the Wardrobe.
    • Trade with other players: /trade opens a window for coins and items, and both sides accept.
    • Your name on it: gear you make says "Forged by" you.

    Bounties & hunts

    • Three bounties wait on your Path every dawn. Finish them before the next for Scrap.
    • Slayer tasks from Kestrel on the island or Vesk in Landfall: hunt a monster, earn Slayer points and XP.
    • Vesk's shop trades points for skips, blocks and the Fang of Vesk.

    Fortune's Dice

    Found in chests, on monsters, in the rock and on the line. Throw one and fate picks a face, from Pocket Change to Mythic Touch.
